About

I’m a Los Angeles-based music writer and creative with a background in technology, storytelling, and a deep love for discovering new sounds. I’m drawn to music that feels cinematic, emotionally honest, and immersive, from alternative and indie to hip-hop, electronic, and everything in between. My goal is to capture not just what an artist sounds like, but the energy, emotion, and experience that make their music worth remembering.

I’m interested in both what music sounds like and how it feels.
